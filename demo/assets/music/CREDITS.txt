The Seventh Ember

Instrument recordings: VSCO 2 Community Edition, Versilian Studios.
Instrument samples are distributed under CC0 1.0. See LICENSE.txt.

The game uses stereo recordings for strings, woodwinds, brass, harp,
marimba, glockenspiel, xylophone, snare, timpani, cymbals, and gong.
The original twelve themes have expanded arrangements, alongside
sixteen additional pieces. See track-list.json for the current score.
